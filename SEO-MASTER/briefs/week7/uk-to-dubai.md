## FAQs (PAA-led, 40 to 90 words each)
**Can I move my dog from the UK to Dubai?**
Yes, if the breed is permitted in the UAE and the paperwork lines up. You need an ISO microchip, a valid rabies vaccination, a MOCCAE import permit (valid 90 days from issuance) and a government-endorsed export health certificate from a UK Official Veterinarian. Dogs and cats arrive into Dubai as manifest cargo. Start with a free eligibility check, then we can quote coordination once the file is clear.
_(68 words)_

**Does a pet from the UK need a rabies titre test for Dubai?**
Usually not, because the UK is treated as a lower-risk rabies origin for UAE import. MOCCAE sets the rule, so confirm on the portal when you apply for the permit. If a titre test is ever required for your file, the blood sample must be taken within 90 days before travel and show at least 0.5 IU/ml. It is never a waiting period after the draw.
_(66 words)_

**How much does it cost to fly a dog from the UK to Dubai?**
There is no honest single figure. The total depends on crate size, airline cargo rates, the vet and certificate work in the UK, and coordination. Government charges are separate: the MOCCAE permit and arrival release fee are published on the MOCCAE portal, so confirm current amounts there. Send us the pet's size and travel month and we quote after the eligibility check.
_(62 words)_

**Can my cat fly in the cabin from London to Dubai?**
Not into Dubai. Emirates states that animals on itineraries ending in Dubai travel as cargo, and most carriers on this corridor follow the same pattern. Etihad publishes a limited cabin option into Abu Dhabi for small pets on some routes, so check current airline policy if that route suits you. Our guide to cabin versus cargo explains the trade-offs.
_(59 words)_

**What documents does a UK pet need to enter Dubai?**
Plan for five items: microchip record, rabies vaccination certificate, MOCCAE import permit issued no more than 90 days before arrival, an export health certificate signed by an Official Veterinarian and endorsed by the UK authority, and the airline cargo booking. Keep originals with the shipment. See the UAE pet import requirements guide for the full Dubai-side list.
_(57 words)_
