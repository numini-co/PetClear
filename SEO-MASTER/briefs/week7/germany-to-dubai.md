## FAQs (PAA-led, 40 to 90 words each)
**Can I fly my dog from Germany to Dubai?**
Yes, if the breed is allowed in the UAE. You need an ISO microchip, valid rabies vaccination, a MOCCAE import permit issued no more than 90 days before arrival and an official veterinary certificate endorsed by the German authorities. Dogs travel into Dubai as manifest cargo, commonly from Frankfurt or Munich. A free eligibility check tells you if anything blocks the file.
_(62 words)_

**Is an EU pet passport enough to enter Dubai?**
No. The EU pet passport records the microchip and rabies vaccine, which helps, but the UAE asks for its own import permit from MOCCAE and an endorsed export health certificate for the flight. Bring the passport as supporting evidence. If you might move back to Germany later, the UAE is on the EU list that is exempt from the titer test for entry.
_(63 words)_
