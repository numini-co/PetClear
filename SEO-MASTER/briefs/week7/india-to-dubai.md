## FAQs (PAA-led, 40 to 90 words each)
**How do I bring a dog to Dubai from India?**
Start about four months out. Your vet implants an ISO microchip and gives a rabies vaccine, then a blood sample goes to an approved lab for a titer test that must show at least 0.5 IU/ml and be taken within 90 days before travel. Next comes the MOCCAE import permit, valid 90 days from issuance, the Indian export certificate and a cargo booking into Dubai.
_(65 words)_

**What is AQCS clearance for pets?**
AQCS is India's Animal Quarantine and Certification Service. For pets it is the official step that checks and certifies animals at the port before they leave or enter India. For a move to Dubai, plan for the export health certificate route at your departure airport. Exact steps vary by station, so confirm with the AQCS office before you book a flight.
_(61 words)_

**How much does pet relocation from India to Dubai cost?**
Costs depend on the titer lab, vet visits, crate size, airline cargo rates and coordination, so we do not publish a package total. Government charges in the UAE, such as the MOCCAE permit and arrival release fee, are listed on the MOCCAE portal and should be confirmed there. Share your pet's details and departure city and we quote after the eligibility check.
_(62 words)_

**Which airlines fly pets from India to Dubai?**
Emirates carries pets into Dubai as SkyCargo shipments, not in the cabin. Air India and other carriers may also accept pets as cargo on some routes, but policies and summer embargoes change often. Confirm current policy with the airline for your departure city. We check live acceptance for Mumbai, Delhi and other hubs once your pet's file is ready.
_(59 words)_
