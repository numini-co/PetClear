## FAQs (PAA-led, 40 to 90 words each)
**How do I move my dog from France to Dubai?**
Check that the breed is allowed in the UAE, then confirm the microchip and rabies vaccination are current. Apply for the MOCCAE import permit, valid 90 days from issuance. A French official vet issues the export health certificate, which is then endorsed by the state veterinary service. Book cargo from Paris or another hub into Dubai, then plan arrival release.
_(60 words)_

**Can my cat fly with me from Paris to Dubai?**
Not in the cabin into Dubai. Emirates carries animals on itineraries ending in Dubai as cargo, and most carriers follow a similar rule. Etihad publishes a limited cabin option into Abu Dhabi for small pets on some routes, so check current policy if that suits you. Otherwise your cat travels as manifest cargo with its own documents.
_(57 words)_
