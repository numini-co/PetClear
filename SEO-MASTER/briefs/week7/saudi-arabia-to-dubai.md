## FAQs (PAA-led, 40 to 90 words each)
**How do I move my dog from Riyadh to Dubai?**
Apply for the UAE import permit on the MOCCAE portal, valid 90 days from issuance, and request the Saudi exit permit through the MEWA Anaam platform. Your vet confirms microchip and rabies vaccination and issues the health certificate. Most owners fly Riyadh to Dubai with the dog as cargo. Check airline acceptance first, because policies and summer limits change.
_(59 words)_

**Do I need a MEWA permit to take my pet out of Saudi Arabia?**
Yes. MEWA issues exit and return permits for pets travelling with their owners through the Anaam platform, and a return must happen within 60 days if you plan to bring the pet back. Arrange this alongside the MOCCAE import permit so both windows overlap with your flight date. We can check the timing in a free eligibility review.
_(58 words)_
