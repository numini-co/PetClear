## FAQs (PAA-led, 40 to 90 words each)
**How do I bring my dog from Singapore to Dubai?**
Confirm the breed is allowed in the UAE, check the microchip and rabies vaccination, and apply for the MOCCAE import permit, valid 90 days from issuance. In Singapore you will also need an export licence and health certificate from the national animal authority before the flight. Most dogs fly from Changi to Dubai as cargo.
_(55 words)_

**Does a pet from Singapore need a titer test for the UAE?**
Usually not, because Singapore is generally treated as a low-risk rabies origin. MOCCAE sets the rule, so confirm on the portal when applying for the permit. If a titer test is ever required, the blood sample must be taken within 90 days before travel and show at least 0.5 IU/ml. Planning a return to Singapore later is a separate, stricter process.
_(61 words)_
