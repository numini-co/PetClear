## 14 content questions (every page answers)

1. **What is this service/process?** One-sentence definition + what this URL is *not*.
2. **Who is it for?** Owner situation, species, direction (to-Dubai vs from-Dubai).
3. **What does it cost?** Itemized types in AED only when `regulatory-source-of-truth.csv` confidence is high. Otherwise verify-on-portal / government-fee-applies. No contested numerals as official facts.
4. **How long does it take?** Scenario timelines (not a single fake SLA).
5. **What documents are needed, in order?** Numbered sequence; chip numbers must match.
6. **What are the eligibility / breed / species constraints?** Link banned-breeds or species pages; do not invent lists.
7. **What can go wrong?** Rejection, expiry, mismatch — process language, not fear copy.
8. **What are the route / airline options?** Link live route or flight-mode pages; no invented cabin products.
9. **What rules apply at origin AND destination?** Two rule-sets; destination-first on export pages.
10. **What happens on arrival?** Clearance, collection, registration — or destination arrival if outbound.
11. **What do I need to do vs what does DPR do?** Coordination only; we do not fly the animal or issue permits.
12. **Why trust DPR?** Verified evidence from `trust-claims-register.csv` only. If unverified, use process language.
13. **FAQs (6–10, unique to this URL)?** No cloned FAQ from a twin. Include FAQPage schema.
14. **What is the next step?** One primary CTA from the taxonomy.
